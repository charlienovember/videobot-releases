Videobot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-07

Videobot is a local desktop application for macOS that turns long-form podcast video into short highlight clips. This policy covers Videobot itself, including its optional feature for connecting a TikTok or Instagram account to publish exported clips.

What stays on your device

All video and audio processing happens locally on your Mac. Videobot does not upload your source recordings, transcripts, or exported clips to any server we operate, and we do not have access to them.

Social account connections

If you choose to connect a TikTok or Instagram account:

  • OAuth tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain, not in a plaintext file, and never leave your device except in direct calls you initiate to TikTok's or Meta's own servers to publish a clip you selected.
  • Non-sensitive account metadata (platform, display name, avatar URL, connection timestamp) is stored in a local SQLite database on your device.
  • Disconnecting an account deletes both the Keychain item and the local database record immediately.

What we don't collect

Videobot does not include analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or any other tracking that sends data about your usage to us or to a third party.

Third parties

The only outside services Videobot talks to are the ones you explicitly connect to (TikTok's and Meta's/Instagram's own APIs), and only to perform the action you request (publishing or scheduling a clip). Each platform's own privacy policy governs how they handle data you send them directly.

Removing your data

Disconnecting a social account removes its stored token and metadata immediately. Uninstalling Videobot removes all locally stored application data, including its databases and any cached exports, from your device.

Questions

Open an issue at github.com/charlienovember/videobot-releases with any privacy questions.
